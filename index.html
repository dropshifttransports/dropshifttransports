FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where are you based and where do you deliver?

We're based in East London and cover all of Greater London, plus deliveries and relocations across England, Scotland and Wales, including Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Bristol, Cardiff, Newcastle, Edinburgh and Glasgow.

Do you work with businesses on a regular basis?

Yes. We support warehouses, retailers, e-commerce businesses, offices and more with occasional jobs, weekly deliveries, scheduled stock transfers and ongoing B2B logistics.

Can you move IT and office equipment?

Yes. We handle office relocations including computers, monitors, printers, server and networking equipment, desks, workstations and filing cabinets.

Do you take single items or small jobs?

Yes. We transport single items and multiple items, from one piece of furniture to a full house or office move.

How do I get a price?

Send us your collection and delivery details using the quote form, WhatsApp or a call. We'll give you a clear, tailored quotation for the job.
