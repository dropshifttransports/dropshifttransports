    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Where are you based and where do you deliver?

    We're based in East London and cover all of Greater London, plus deliveries and relocations across England, Scotland and Wales, including Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Bristol, Cardiff, Newcastle, Edinburgh and Glasgow.

    Do you work with businesses on a regular basis?

    Yes. We support warehouses, retailers, e-commerce businesses, offices and more with occasional jobs, weekly deliveries, scheduled stock transfers and ongoing B2B logistics.

    Can you move IT and office equipment?

    Yes. We handle office relocations including computers, monitors, printers, server and networking equipment, desks, workstations and filing cabinets.

    Do you take single items or small jobs?

    Yes. We transport single items and multiple items, from one piece of furniture to a full house or office move.

    How do I get a price?

    Send us your collection and delivery details using the quote form, WhatsApp or a call. We'll give you a clear, tailored quotation for the job — including man and van prices and house removal quotes.

    Do you offer man and van hire?

    Yes. We provide man and van London services — local man with a van, man and van for furniture, two-man teams and same-day options by arrangement. Man and van details.

    Do you do same-day removals or courier work?

    Same-day removals, urgent man and van and same day courier jobs are available across Greater London when diaries allow. Call or WhatsApp for instant availability.

    Do your vans have a tail lift?

    Yes. Our Luton vans are equipped with a tail lift and pallet truck for palletised stock and commercial deliveries. See pallet delivery UK.

    Are you insured?

    Yes. We are a fully insured removal company with goods-in-transit and public liability cover — amounts are available on request.
