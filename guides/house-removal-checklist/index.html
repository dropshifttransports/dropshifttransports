House removal checklist

Use this checklist before moving day so your house removals run smoothly.

Get a quote WhatsApp

Declutter early

Sell or donate items you will not move — smaller volume means a lower house removal cost.

Pack room by room

Label boxes clearly. Keep essentials (kettle, chargers, documents) in a separate day bag.

Measure big items

Sofas, beds and fridges — note stairs and door widths so we plan two-man delivery if needed.

Book parking / permits

Arrange loading space at both addresses where possible, especially in East London.

Confirm utilities & keys

Meter readings, broadband, and key handover times synced with your removal slot.

Book DropShift

Send your house removal quote request with dates and a rough inventory — we confirm crew and van size.
